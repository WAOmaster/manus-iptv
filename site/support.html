How do I add a playlist?

Open Add playlist in the app. Either type the M3U link with the remote, or choose the phone option, scan the QR code with your phone (same Wi-Fi) and paste the link there.

Which remote keys work?

  • Arrows: move; OK: select or show channel info.
  • Up/Down while watching: change channel.
  • Left/Right: rewind or forward where the stream allows it.
  • Back: close the current screen; Menu: options.

A channel will not play

  • Check that the playlist link still works in a browser.
  • Some streams are offline or limited to certain countries; try another version of the channel.
  • Check the TV's internet connection.

No subtitle translation

Translation models download once from Google. Make sure the TV is online and has free storage.

Is content included?

No. The app is only a player. You provide the playlists and must have the right to use them.
